    Assumed Counter

    Counter cards are discarded once played, so this budget is shared and spent down across every attack this turn, the same way DON!! is - but it can also grow mid-turn: a Life card dealt as damage is added to hand unless it's Banished, and that card is immediately available to Counter a later battle in the same sequence. The search below accounts for that growth, and for the Active Blockers count above, when checking whether a line survives.

    Counter Events

    A Counter Event is a card played from hand for a special effect instead of a printed Counter value - counted separately from the generic 1000/2000 cards above. Each one below needs its own hand card, on top of whatever's used for generic Counter.

    Costs 1 hand card + 1 DON!! for +4000 power. Available to every color - most decks run 2-4 copies, Purple decks typically run more, around 4-6.

    Costs 2 hand cards (the event itself, plus discarding another card) for +3000 power, no DON!! needed. Has no effect unless Leader color (primary or secondary) is Red. Red decks typically run 2-4 copies.

Has no effect unless Leader color (primary or secondary) is Yellow. Yellow decks typically run 4-8 copies - the calculator estimates how many show up in the current Life total from this count and your deck size above.

    Active Blockers

    How many active (not rested) Characters they have that could block - each can only block once per turn. You don't assign which attack each one blocks: since blocking never costs Life either way in this tool, the calculator works out the best way to spend your Blockers (and Counter) together to survive, the same way it already does for Counter alone.

    Find lethal

    Just list who's attacking above - the calculator searches your DON!! split AND the order to attack in (order can change whether a Life card an earlier hit reveals funds Countering a later, pricier attack), plus (given how many active Blockers you entered above) which attacks the opponent blocks and which they Counter. The recommended order for each line is shown in its results below.

    How the percentage is calculated: each line is checked against thousands of random hands and Life piles, drawn from the deck details you enter below (hand size, 1000-/2000-power Counter cards, Counter Events, Yellow's heal-Trigger count, deck size) - not one average guess. For each simulated hand, the calculator works out the best way the opponent could combine their Counter, Blockers, and (for Yellow) healing to survive, the same way a real opponent would; the percentage is how often that fails. DON!! and Blocker counts are entered directly rather than sampled, since those are known board state, not hidden information. What's still approximated: a Life card dealt as damage mid-turn grows the Counter budget available for later battles using the deck's average Counter value, not a fully re-sampled draw; the discard cost for Red's Event and Yellow's heal is assumed to come from non-Counter cards whenever possible; and generic Trigger effects beyond Yellow's heal-on-Trigger still aren't modeled - what an un-Banished Life card does if revealed needs rules only you can supply.

    What's deliberately left out of the Counter Events above: Purple's Slow Slow Beam (2 DON!!, +2000 Counter, and rests an enemy Character) isn't modeled - unlike every other Counter effect here, resting a Character can cancel a LATER attack in your line outright rather than just adding defensive power to one battle, which would need the attack-resolution loop itself to support a battle being called off mid-sequence rather than just negated. Dual-color Leaders get both of their colors' Counter Events, but no deeper two-color interactions are modeled yet. Blue and Black have no color-specific mechanic to model - Blue's larger boards and heavier Blocker count are already captured by the Active Blockers count above. Green is the same for now; a Leader releasing in about a month is expected to let certain cards count as 3000 Counter, but it isn't in the game yet.
