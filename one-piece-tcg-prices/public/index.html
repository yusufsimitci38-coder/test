    Plans out one turn's worth of attacks and tells you whether it's lethal, accounting for Double Attack (counts as two separate Life hits, each a separate chance at a Trigger), Banish (the Life card is trashed unseen - no Trigger at all), Blocker, DON!!, and K.O. comparisons (ties go to the attacker). Set how many DON!! you have available this turn and it searches every way to split them across your attackers, surfacing which splits are lethal instead of making you test one allocation at a time - and instead of guessing a Counter value per attack, it estimates the opponent's total Counter budget from their hand size and deck composition. This is a deterministic calculator, not a true probability engine yet - see the note at the bottom for why, and what's needed to get there.

    Assumed Counter

    Only modeled for attacks aimed directly at the Leader - Counter spent saving a Blocker never changes whether the turn is lethal, so it isn't tracked here. Counter cards are discarded once played, so this budget is shared and spent down across every attack this turn, the same way DON!! is - but it can also grow mid-turn: a Life card dealt as damage is added to hand unless it's Banished, and that card is immediately available to Counter a later battle in the same sequence. The search below accounts for that growth when checking whether a line survives.

    Attack order

    Attacks resolve top to bottom - a Blocker used earlier in the list is rested and unavailable for a later attack targeting it (the calculator will flag this and treat that attack as unblocked instead). Drag isn't supported yet; use the ↑/↓ buttons on each row above to reorder. Targets and attack order are fixed as you set them above - only the DON!! split across attackers is searched.

    Why this isn't a real probability yet: a true P(lethal) needs to account for hidden information - the defender's actual hand, and what an un-Banished Life card's Trigger would do. Counter is now estimated instead of guessed: enter the opponent's hand size plus how many 1000- and 2000-power Counter cards their deck runs, and the calculator averages that into an assumed total Counter budget for the turn (Counter cards are discarded once played, so like DON!!, it's one shared pool spent down across every attack - not a fresh value per battle - and it grows mid-turn as non-Banished Life cards reach hand from earlier hits in the same sequence). Results are split into lines that are lethal even against that full assumed budget versus lines that only connect if the opponent is short on Counter, so you can see how risky each plan actually is. Generic Trigger effects still aren't modeled - what an un-Banished Life card does if revealed needs rules only you can supply (Yellow's heal-on-Trigger cards are the one specific case that's covered, since it was described precisely enough to estimate). Likewise, the "N% of DON!! allocations are lethal" figure next to your results isn't a real probability either - you're choosing how to split your DON!!, not drawing it at random - it's a coverage stat showing how many of the ways you could spend your pool actually win, with the cheapest winning split called out for you to play.

    What's deliberately left out of the Counter Events above: Purple's Slow Slow Beam (2 DON!!, +2000 Counter, and rests an enemy Character) isn't modeled - unlike every other Counter effect here, resting a Character can cancel a LATER attack in your line outright rather than just adding defensive power to one battle, which would need the attack-resolution loop itself to support a battle being called off mid-sequence rather than just negated. Dual-color Leaders get both of their colors' Counter Events, but no deeper two-color interactions are modeled yet, and Green, Blue, and Black currently only get the universal DON!!-based Counter Event until their specific decks are described.
